Before we begin

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Everything you need to know before starting a character or animation project.

What do I need to start?+

A short idea, reference images if you have them, and where the character will be used. We’ll help shape the rest.

Which files will I receive?+

Deliverables are agreed before work begins and can include PNG, JPG, layered PSD or AI, animation-ready assets and final video formats.

Can you work from my existing sketch?+

Yes. Upload your sketch or source artwork in the project brief, then attach it when WhatsApp opens.

How does feedback work?+

Each milestone is shared clearly so feedback stays focused and the final delivery matches the approved direction.
